About Jaipur

Jaipur, known as the Pink City, is the capital of Rajasthan. It is famous for its magnificent forts, royal palaces, traditional markets, colourful culture and historic architecture.

🏰 Forts

Explore impressive forts and historic structures that showcase the royal heritage of Rajasthan.

👑 Royal Heritage

Experience beautiful palaces, traditional architecture and the history of Rajasthan's royal families.

🛍️ Markets

Discover colourful bazaars, handicrafts, traditional clothing and local products.

Jaipur Highlights

  • 🏰 Historic forts and magnificent palaces
  • 🌸 Famous Pink City architecture
  • 👑 Rich royal history and culture
  • 🛍️ Traditional markets and handicrafts
  • 🍛 Delicious Rajasthani cuisine
  • 📸 Beautiful photography locations
